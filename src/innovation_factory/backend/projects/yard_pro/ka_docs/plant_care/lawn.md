# Lawn care, Stuttgart region

A residential lawn in the Stuttgart kettle is usually a seed mix of
perennial ryegrass, smooth-stalked meadow grass, and fine fescues
(Spielrasen or Zierrasen). The four levers that determine lawn quality
are mowing height, watering depth, fertilizer timing, and an annual
scarification + overseed pass in spring or early autumn.

## Mowing height

Mowing height is the single most underused lever in residential lawn
care. The general rule is: **never remove more than a third of the
grass blade in one cut**, and keep the lawn taller in heat.

- **Spring and autumn** — 4 cm cut height.
- **Summer heat** (above ~25 °C ambient) — 5 to 6 cm cut height. Taller
  grass shades its own roots, reduces evaporation, and is far more
  resilient to drought.
- **First cut of the season** — wait until the lawn is ~7-8 cm tall,
  then cut to 5 cm. A robotic mower that mows daily achieves this
  effortlessly; for a rotary mower, set the deck a notch higher than
  feels right.

## Watering depth

Shallow daily watering is worse than no watering — it trains roots to
stay near the surface, where they are vulnerable to summer drought. The
better rhythm is one or two **deep** soaks per week. Deep means roughly
20-25 litres per square metre per session, enough to wet the top 10-15
cm of soil. Water in the early morning to minimise evaporation and to
let the blades dry before nightfall (wet blades overnight encourage
fungal disease).

## Fertilizer

Three applications per season for a typical Stuttgart lawn:

- **Late March / early April** — high-nitrogen spring fertilizer (NPK
  around 20-5-8) to kick-start growth.
- **Mid-June** — balanced summer fertilizer (NPK around 15-5-15) to
  maintain colour through the heat.
- **Mid-September** — potassium-heavy autumn fertilizer (NPK around
  6-5-20) to harden the lawn for winter.

Always apply fertilizer to a dry lawn, then water in. Granules left on
wet blades will scorch the lawn.

## Scarification and overseed

A vertical scarification pass with a fine-rake or scarifier in late
March or early April removes thatch and opens the soil to air and
overseed. Overseed bare patches with the same seed mix immediately
after scarifying; water lightly and consistently for two weeks until
the new seed germinates.

---

*This guide is advisory only. Lawn quality is highly local; if a
patch is yellowing, browning in patches, or showing fungal rings,
photograph the area and check the diagnostic playbooks before
intervening.*
