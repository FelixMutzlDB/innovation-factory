# Copper fungicide — application windows, dilution, tolerance

Copper-based fungicides (copper hydroxide, copper oxychloride, copper
sulphate, Bordeaux mixture) are the standard preventative for several
fruit-tree and ornamental fungal diseases. In a Stuttgart garden, the
most common use case is a dormant late-winter application on apples,
plums, and cherries to reduce scab, peach-leaf curl, and pocket-plum
pressure for the coming season.

## When to apply

Copper sprays are **preventative**, not curative. Once visible
infection has appeared, copper will not cure it. The right application
windows are:

- **Dormant application** — late February to early March, before bud
  break. This is the highest-impact single spray for fruit trees in
  Stuttgart. Choose a calm, dry day with no rain forecast for at least
  24 hours.
- **Bud-burst application** — only if pressure was high in the
  previous season. Same plant, second light application as buds just
  begin to break colour.
- **Avoid applications during bloom** — copper is toxic to bees during
  flowering. Always check the product label.

## Dilution and dose

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions on the bottle. Typical
home-garden dilution rates are in the range of 10-30 g of product per
10 L of water, but exact numbers vary by formulation; the wrong
dilution does damage. A spray to the point of run-off is correct —
just to the start of dripping, not soaking the bark.

## Plant tolerance

Copper is tough on tissue, especially young, soft tissue. Tolerance:

- **High tolerance:** apple, plum, cherry, currant (dormant only).
- **Moderate tolerance:** grape, peach.
- **Low tolerance:** roses, tomato seedlings, ornamental annuals.
  Avoid copper on these unless directly directed by the product label.

Repeated copper applications also accumulate in the soil over years
and can affect earthworms and beneficial microorganisms. Use copper
sprays sparingly — once a year at most in normal pressure conditions.

## Mixing safety

- Always mix in cold water in a labelled, dedicated sprayer. Wash the
  sprayer thoroughly after use.
- Wear gloves, long sleeves, and eye protection.
- Mix only the amount you will use in one session; copper mixtures
  destabilise within hours.
- Never mix copper with other products unless the label explicitly
  states compatibility.

## Disposal

Unused mixed product cannot be poured down the drain. Apply unused
material to a non-target area (gravel, unplanted soil) or dispose
through municipal hazardous-waste collection.

## Storage

Store dry, in original packaging, away from food, frost-free, away
from children and animals. Shelf life of dry concentrate is multiple
years; mixed solution is hours.

*This guide is advisory only. Copper fungicides are restricted in
several EU jurisdictions; always confirm current legal status and
follow the manufacturer's label exactly. When in doubt, consult an
agricultural advisory service or a licensed crop-care specialist.*
