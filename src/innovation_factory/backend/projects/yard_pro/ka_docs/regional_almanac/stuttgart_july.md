# Stuttgart — July garden tasks

July is high summer in the Stuttgart kettle. Lawns, roses, and
established trees move firmly into a defensive mode: deep watering on
the dry weeks, no major pruning during heat-waves, and a careful eye on
fungal pressure during the inevitable thunderstorm runs. Most of the
month's headline jobs come in the last week, when summer cherry pruning
opens and lavender finishes its first bloom.

## Weekend-by-weekend planner

### First weekend (3-5 July)

- Apple-tree fruit-thinning window is still open; finish thinning to
  one fruit per spur if not already done.
- Rose second flush approaching. Continue base-only watering on dry
  weeks; keep foliage dry to discourage black spot.
- Box-tree moth: second-generation flight peaks; the inspection
  rhythm tightens to weekly.

### Second weekend (10-12 July)

- Final window for summer-prune apple light shaping if you skipped
  late June; do not exceed 20% canopy removal in summer.
- Lavender approaching peak bloom. Note that the shaping prune is
  *after* bloom, not during.
- Cherry harvest is finishing for most early-mid varieties. Net trees
  on remaining late varieties.

### Third weekend (17-19 July)

- **Cherry summer pruning window opens** — late July through early
  September is the only correct window. Pick a dry week; remove
  diseased, broken, or crossing branches; cut just outside the branch
  collar; never in winter (silver-leaf risk).
- Roses: continue deadheading. Watch for the first signs of powdery
  mildew on lower foliage.

### Fourth weekend (24-26 July)

- **Lavender post-bloom shaping prune** — once the first bloom is fully
  past. Remove spent flower stalks plus the top 1-2 cm of green
  foliage; **never** cut into woody old growth. Keep the cushion form.
- Lawn watering on dry weeks: one deep weekly soak (20-25 L / m²)
  applied in the early morning. Do not water in the evening — wet
  blades overnight encourage fungal disease.
- Late-cherry summer prune continues.

### Fifth weekend (31 July - 2 August)

- Plum prune window approaching (late July to early September). Same
  rule as cherry: dry week, clean cuts, branch collar respected.
- Rose second flush beginning to peak. Light shaping cuts are safe;
  no hard pruning.

## Watering decisions

July in the Stuttgart kettle frequently crosses the dry-and-above-28 °C
threshold for a week or more at a time. The rhythm: deep weekly soak
for established lawn and beds; deeper, twice-weekly soak for hydrangea
and any newly planted woody species; lavender remains on its own
schedule of once every 2-3 weeks at most.

## Watch-fors

- **Late blight on tomatoes** — yellow leaf edges, dark stem lesions.
  Remove affected leaves, ventilate plants, water at the base only.
- **Apple scab** — circular dark olive lesions on leaves and fruit.
  Sanitation matters: remove fallen leaves; treat early in stressful
  weather.
- **Heat-stressed lawn yellowing** — usually a height issue, not a
  fertilizer issue. Raise the cutting height before reaching for
  fertilizer.

---

*This planner is advisory only. July weather in the Stuttgart kettle
is variable; always check the local seven-day forecast before
fertilizer applications or heavy summer pruning.*
